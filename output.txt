---Loss Function---

Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) =  – ( y * log(y_pred) + (1 – y) * log(1 – y_pred) )

BCE: dofference between predicted probability and actual probability

y: True label (either 0 or 1 (positive and negative in our use))

y_pred: Predicted probability (between 0 and 1 (positive or negative))

log: Natural logarithm
